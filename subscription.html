Questions

Can I pause a week?

Yes — just text us by Wednesday evening and that Friday is skipped, no charge. Cancel anytime the same way.

Where do you deliver?

Across the Raleigh–Durham–Cary area (the Triangle), Friday afternoons. Outside the area? Ask us — we often find a way.

We have allergies. Can we still subscribe?

Yes. Tell us in the form and we rotate your flavors around them. Every pie's ingredients and allergens are listed on its card on the pies page.

Can I choose the flavors?

The rotation is ours — that's the fun of it. But tell us your loves and no-gos, and we plan your Fridays around them.
