Our story

Bite into our bestВіtе іntо оur bеѕt

In 2021 we arrived here all on our own — no grandmas, no grandpas nearby. So we made our own warmth: a family tradition of baking pies in the evenings, with fruit tea and milk. We watched fairy tales, we played board games, and the whole home filled with the smell of butter and berries. Every single time it felt cozy — and unbelievably tasty!

The girls joined in so quickly — and now they bake their own pies and experiment all the time. My husband always told me my pies were wonderful, and that we should sell them. So finally — we did.
